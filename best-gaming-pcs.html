PC Building Tips

  • ✔ Always prioritize the graphics card for gaming performance.
  • ✔ Install games on an NVMe SSD for much faster loading times.
  • ✔ Use dual-channel RAM (2 sticks instead of 1).
  • ✔ Buy a high-quality power supply from a trusted brand.
  • ✔ Ensure your PC case has at least three cooling fans for proper airflow.
  • ✔ Choose upgrade-friendly components so you can replace the GPU or add more RAM later.

Final Verdict

The HP OMEN 35L is the best premium option, the Lenovo Legion Tower 5 offers the best balance of performance and price, and the Acer Nitro 50 is a solid choice for budget-conscious gamers looking for smooth 1080p gameplay.
